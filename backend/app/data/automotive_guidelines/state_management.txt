TOPIC: State Management
CATEGORY: STATE_MANAGEMENT
APPLICABLE_VULNERABILITIES: STATE_MANAGEMENT, race condition, TOCTOU
REFERENCE: CERT C Secure Coding CON43-C
SOURCE: automotive_guidelines/state_management.txt

GUIDANCE:
Ensure thread safety when accessing shared state. Use appropriate synchronization primitives like mutexes.
Avoid reliance on global variables. If globals are necessary, strictly control their access.
Prevent Time-of-Check to Time-of-Use (TOCTOU) race conditions by locking resources during the check and use sequence.

RATIONALE:
Inconsistent state in concurrent automotive environments can lead to unpredictable actuator behavior or system crashes. Safe state management is consistent with MISRA-oriented safe coding practices.
