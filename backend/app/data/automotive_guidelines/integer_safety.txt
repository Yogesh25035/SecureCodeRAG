TOPIC: Integer Safety
CATEGORY: INTEGER_SAFETY
APPLICABLE_VULNERABILITIES: INTEGER_OVERFLOW, integer underflow, sign extension
REFERENCE: CERT C Secure Coding INT30-C, INT32-C
SOURCE: automotive_guidelines/integer_safety.txt

GUIDANCE:
Validate all arithmetic operations to ensure they do not result in integer overflow or underflow.
Be cautious of implicit type conversions and sign extensions.
Ensure buffer size calculations and loop counters use appropriate data types (e.g., size_t).

RATIONALE:
Integer overflows can lead to incorrect buffer size allocations or bypassed bounds checks, leading to severe memory corruption. Ensuring integer safety is consistent with MISRA-oriented safe coding practices.
